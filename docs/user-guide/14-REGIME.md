# Market Regime Center

## Purpose

The Market Regime Center tells you **what kind of market it is right now**. This is like checking the weather before you go outside — you wouldn't go hiking in a thunderstorm, and you shouldn't use certain strategies in the wrong market.

**Think of it like a weather forecast for the stock market.** It tells you whether today is sunny (calm uptrend), stormy (high volatility), or foggy (no clear direction).

---

## Who Should Use It

| User | Why |
|---|---|
| **Beginners** | To understand if today is a good day to trade |
| **Everyone** | To decide which strategy to use |

---

## The 14 Market Regimes

The AI detects **14 different market conditions**:

| Regime | What It Looks Like | What To Do |
|---|---|---|
| **Strong Bull Trend** | Market going UP strongly | Look for buying opportunities |
| **Strong Bear Trend** | Market going DOWN strongly | Look for selling opportunities |
| **Weak Bull Trend** | Market going up, but weakly | Be careful with buys |
| **Weak Bear Trend** | Market going down, but weakly | Be careful with sells |
| **Sideways Range** | Price going nowhere | Wait or use range strategy |
| **High Volatility** | Prices moving wildly | Be very careful |
| **Low Volatility** | Very quiet market | May not be worth trading |
| **Breakout** | Price breaking through a level | Good for breakout strategy |
| **Fake Breakout** | Price broke out then reversed | DO NOT trade breakouts |
| **Mean Reversion** | Price has moved too far | Expect a pullback |
| **News Driven** | Market moving on news | Unpredictable — avoid |
| **Opening Auction** | First 30 minutes of trading | Wait for settled prices |
| **Closing Session** | Last 30 minutes of trading | Be careful, positions closing |
| **Illiquid Market** | Very few people trading | Avoid — hard to exit trades |

---

## How to Read the Regime Display

| Element | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Current Regime** | The detected market condition |
| **Regime Confidence** | How sure the AI is (0-100%) |
| **Stability** | How stable this regime is (higher = more reliable) |
| **Duration** | How long this regime has lasted |
| **Previous Regime** | What the market was doing before |

---

## Strategy Recommendation

For each regime, the AI recommends:

| Regime | Primary Strategy | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Strong Bull Trend | Trend Following | Reversal, Range |
| Strong Bear Trend | Trend Following | Reversal, Range |
| Sideways Range | Range Strategy | Trend Following |
| High Volatility | Momentum | Range, Scalping |
| Low Volatility | No Trade | Most strategies |
| Breakout | Breakout | Range, Reversal |
| Fake Breakout | Mean Reversion | Breakout, Momentum |
| News Driven | No Trade | ALL strategies |

---

## Stability Meter

The Stability Meter shows how reliable the current regime detection is:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 70-100% | Stable — you can trust this regime |
| 🟡 40-70% | Moderate — keep watching |
| 🔴 0-40% | Unstable — regime may change soon |

If stability is low, the market is likely **transitioning** between regimes.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why |
|---|---|
| Trading a breakout in a range market | Breakouts fail in ranges |
| Using trend strategies in sideways markets | You will buy high and sell low |
| Ignoring regime changes | The market can flip suddenly |
| Trading during illiquid periods | You may not be able to exit |
