# First Trade Guide (Paper Trading)

## Before You Start

This guide walks you through placing your **first paper trade**.

**What is paper trading?** It is trading with fake money in a real market. The prices are real. Your money is not.

**Why paper trade first?** Because mistakes are free. You learn without losing money.

---

## Step 1: Go to Paper Trading

Click **"Paper Trading"** in the left sidebar.

This opens the Paper Trading Dashboard. You will see:

- **Account Balance** — Your fake money (starts at ₹1,00,000)
- **Controls** — Start, Pause, Stop buttons
- **Positions** — Any open trades (empty for now)
- **Orders** — Any pending orders (empty for now)
- **Trades** — Your trade history (empty for now)

---

## Step 2: Start Paper Trading

Click the **"Start"** button.

The status will change from STOPPED to RUNNING.

The paper trading engine is now active. It monitors the market and generates trades based on AI analysis.

---

## Step 3: Check the AI Decision

Go to the **Dashboard** or **AI Decision Center**.

Check:

1. **What does the AI say?** BUY, SELL, or NO TRADE?
2. **What is the Confidence?** Above 80 is ideal.
3. **What is the Trade Grade?** B or higher is good.
4. **Do the approval gates pass?** All green checkmarks.

---

## Step 4: Review the Trade Plan

If the AI says BUY or SELL, it also creates a **Trade Plan**:

| Plan Element | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Direction** | BUY or SELL |
| **Entry Price** | The price the AI wants to enter |
| **Stop Loss (SL)** | The price where the AI will exit if wrong |
| **Target** | The price where the AI expects profit |
| **Risk/Reward** | How much you risk vs how much you could gain |

**Example:**
- Entry: ₹25,100
- Stop Loss: ₹24,800 (risk ₹300)
- Target: ₹25,700 (reward ₹600)
- Risk/Reward: **1:2** (risk 1 to gain 2)

---

## Step 5: Review Risk

Go to **Risk Center**.

Check:

- **Risk Score:** Should be LOW or MEDIUM
- **Daily Loss:** Should be under the limit
- **Exposure:** Should be under 60%
- **Kill Switch:** Should be OFF
- **Trading Allowed:** Should say YES

---

## Step 6: Monitor the Trade

Once the paper trade is placed:

- **Watch it** from the Paper Trading dashboard
- **Check the P&L** — Is it winning or losing?
- **Let it run** — Do not close early unless the AI says so
- **Wait for exit** — The AI will close at SL or Target

---

## Step 7: Review After Closing

When the trade closes (either at SL or Target):

1. Go to **Performance Analytics**
2. Find the trade in the trade list
3. Check the **Trade Score** (0-100)
4. Read the **Outcome Classification**:

| Classification | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Excellent | Everything went perfectly |
| Good | Good trade, small improvement possible |
| Average | Could have been better |
| Poor | Something went wrong |
| Failed | The trade lost money |

---

## Common Mistakes (Paper Trading)

| Mistake | Why |
|---|---|
| Taking trades below 80% confidence | You are gambling, not trading |
| Closing trades early | Let the AI manage the exit |
| Trading during red risk | High risk = high chance of loss |
| Overtrading | 1-2 good trades beat 10 mediocre ones |
| Ignoring SL | Stop Loss is your safety net |

---

## Pro Tips

- **Start small.** Even with paper money, treat it like real money.
- **Track every trade.** Note what you learned.
- **Focus on process, not profit.** Good process → good results.
- **If you lose 3 in a row, stop.** Review what went wrong.
