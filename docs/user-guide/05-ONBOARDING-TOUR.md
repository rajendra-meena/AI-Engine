# Interactive Onboarding Tour

A step-by-step guided tour of the entire platform. Follow these steps in order to learn the system completely.

---

## Step 1: Welcome Screen

**What:** The Dashboard

**What to do:**
1. Open the app
2. Look at the Dashboard
3. Find the AI Decision panel on the right
4. Notice what the AI is saying (BUY/SELL/NO TRADE)

**You learned:** This is your home base. Every session starts here.

---

## Step 2: The AI Decision

**What:** AI Decision panel

**What to do:**
1. Look at the **Confidence** number
2. Look at the **Score** number
3. Read the **Reasoning** text
4. If available, click the **Explain** tab

**You learned:** The AI tells you what it thinks AND why.

---

## Step 3: The Market Regime

**What:** Look for the Regime indicator (may be a badge on the dashboard or the Regime Center page)

**What to do:**
1. Note the current regime name
2. Check the regime confidence
3. See which strategy is recommended

**You learned:** The market has a "personality" today. The AI adjusts its strategy accordingly.

---

## Step 4: Risk Check

**What:** Risk Center

**What to do:**
1. Look at the Risk Score
2. Check if trading is allowed
3. Verify the Kill Switch is OFF
4. Check your Daily Loss

**You learned:** Always check risk before trading.

---

## Step 5: Paper Trading

**What:** Paper Trading Dashboard

**What to do:**
1. Click **Start** to begin paper trading
2. Switch back to Dashboard and wait for AI signals
3. When a signal appears, review the Trade Plan
4. Watch the trade progress in the bottom panel

**You learned:** Paper trading lets you practice without risk.

---

## Step 6: The Bottom Panel

**What:** The panel at the bottom of every page

**What to do:**
1. Notice the tabs: Logs, Orders, Trades, Positions
2. Click different tabs to see their content
3. Watch the Logs tab for real-time messages
4. Resize the panel by dragging the top edge

**You learned:** The bottom panel shows live activity across the system.

---

## Step 7: The Chart

**What:** Main chart on the Dashboard

**What to do:**
1. Observe the candlesticks (green = up, red = down)
2. Notice any lines drawn by the AI (support/resistance)
3. Change the timeframe if controls are visible

**You learned:** The chart shows price history. The AI reads it for you.

---

## Step 8: Review Performance

**What:** AI Performance Center

**What to do:**
1. Look at the Overall Trade Score
2. Check the Win Rate
3. See which strategies performed best
4. Review any mistakes the AI identified

**You learned:** The system tracks its own performance and learns from mistakes.

---

## Step 9: Check Health

**What:** Command Center

**What to do:**
1. Check the Unified Status
2. Verify all subsystems are healthy
3. Check for any incidents
4. Note the data age

**You learned:** The system monitors its own health continuously.

---

## Step 10: Explore (Your Turn)

**What:** Any page you haven't visited yet

**Try:**
- Market Regime Center
- AI Performance Center
- Model Governance Center
- Production Readiness
- Strategy pages

**You learned:** The platform has many specialized pages. Explore them at your own pace.

---

## Tour Complete!

You have now seen every major feature of MarketMind AI.

**Remember:**
- ✅ Start at the Dashboard
- ✅ Check the AI Decision
- ✅ Verify Risk before trading
- ✅ Review performance regularly
- ✅ The more you use it, the more you learn
