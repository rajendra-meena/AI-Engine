# First Day Guide

## Welcome to Your First Day

You have opened MarketMind AI for the first time. You see a screen with charts, numbers, and buttons. You do not know what any of it means.

**That is completely normal.** This guide will walk you through your first 30 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Just Look Around

**Do not click anything yet.**

Take 30 seconds to look at what you see:

| Area | What It Is |
|---|---|
| **Left Sidebar** | The table of contents — every page in this app |
| **Main Area** | The page you are currently on |
| **Bottom Panel** | Live information — logs, orders, trades |
| **Right Panel** | AI analysis — what the AI is thinking |

---

## Step 2: Go to the Dashboard

Click **"Dashboard"** in the left sidebar.

This is your home screen. It shows:

- **AI Decision** — What the AI thinks about the current market
- **Market Overview** — Key numbers at a glance
- **Chart** — Price movements (you don't need to understand this yet)

---

## Step 3: Watch the AI Decision Panel

On the right side of the screen, you will see a panel labeled **AI Decision**.

It shows one of these:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **BUY** | The AI sees an opportunity to buy |
| **SELL** | The AI sees an opportunity to sell |
| **NO TRADE** | The AI thinks it is better to wait |
| **WAIT** | Not enough information yet |

Below the decision, you will see:

- **Confidence** — How sure the AI is (0% = not sure, 100% = very sure)
- **Score** — How good the setup looks (0 = bad, 100 = excellent)

---

## Step 4: Ignore Most Things

On your first day, ignore:

- Charts (just look at them)
- Risk metrics (the AI handles this)
- Settings (defaults are fine)
- Model governance (advanced feature)
- Production readiness (for later)

**What to focus on:**

1. The AI Decision panel
2. The Confidence number
3. Whether it says BUY, SELL, or NO TRADE

---

## Step 5: Change the Symbol

At the top of the screen, there is a symbol selector showing "NIFTY 50".

Click it and try different symbols:

- NIFTY 50 — India's main index
- BANKNIFTY — Banking stocks index
- SENSEX — Another Indian index

Watch how the AI's decision changes for different symbols.

---

## Step 6: Read the Explanation

Click the **Explain** tab in the AI panel (or go to the Explainability Center).

The AI will tell you WHY it made its decision in plain English:

- "Trend is strong bullish"
- "Volume is above average"
- "RSI is in normal range"

Do not worry about these terms yet. Just read them.

---

## Step 7: Close for the Day

That is enough for your first day.

Tomorrow, come back and:

1. Check if the AI says the same thing
2. Notice how the confidence changes
3. Read the explanation again

---

## What NOT to Do on Day 1

| Action | Why Not |
|---|---|
| Place a real trade | You need to learn paper trading first |
| Change settings | Default settings are safe |
| Ignore "NO TRADE" | If the AI says no, trust it |
| Try to beat the AI | First learn what it does |
| Click every button | You will learn them one at a time |
