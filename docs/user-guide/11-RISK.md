# Risk Center

## Purpose

The Risk Center is your **safety shield**. It monitors everything that could go wrong and stops bad trades before they happen.

**Think of it like a lifeguard at a swimming pool.** The lifeguard does not stop you from swimming. But if you are about to swim into dangerous water, they blow the whistle.

---

## Who Should Use It

| User | Why |
|---|---|
| **Beginners** | Check this before every trade |
| **Everyone** | Must review when Risk turns RED |

---

## Understanding Risk Score

The Risk Center shows a single number called **Risk Score**:

| Score | Color | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0-30 | 🟢 Green | Safe — normal market conditions |
| 30-50 | 🟡 Yellow | Caution — be more careful |
| 50-70 | 🟠 Orange | Warning — consider waiting |
| 70-100 | 🔴 Red | Danger — do NOT trade |

---

## Features

### Daily Loss Limit

- Tracks how much you have lost today
- When it hits the limit, trading stops automatically
- **Purpose:** prevents a bad day from becoming a disaster

### Exposure Monitor

- Shows how much of your money is at risk
- Higher exposure = higher risk
- **Rule of thumb:** Keep exposure below 60%

### Drawdown Tracker

- Drawdown = how far your account has fallen from its highest point
- **Example:** If you had ₹1,00,000 and now have ₹85,000, your drawdown is 15%
- High drawdown means you need to be more careful

### Kill Switch

The **big red button** that stops everything immediately.

| Switch State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| OFF 🟢 | Normal trading |
| ON 🔴 | ALL trading stopped. Only a human can restart. |

**When to use Kill Switch:**
- You notice something very wrong
- The market is behaving unexpectedly
- You want to stop everything RIGHT NOW

### Trading Allowed Status

| Status | What It Means |
|---|---|
| ✅ Trading Allowed | You can place trades |
| ❌ Trading Blocked | Something is preventing trades |

**Common reasons for "Trading Blocked":**
- Daily loss limit reached
- Risk score too high
- Drawdown too high
- Kill switch is active
- Market data is stale

## Color Guide

| Element | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Risk Score | 0-30 | 30-50 | 50+ |
| Daily Loss | Under limit | Approaching limit | Hit limit |
| Exposure | Under 60% | 60-80% | Over 80% |
| Drawdown | Under 10% | 10-20% | Over 20% |
| Kill Switch | Off | — | Active |

---

## Beginner Questions

**Q: Can I trade when Risk is Yellow?**

Yes, but be more careful than usual. Consider smaller trade size.

**Q: What happens when Risk is Red?**

The system may block your trade. This is for your protection.

**Q: Can I override the Risk Center?**

No. The Risk Center has final authority. This prevents catastrophic losses.

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why |
|---|---|
| Ignoring yellow warnings | Yellow often turns red |
| Not checking daily loss | You might hit the limit mid-trade |
| Keeping high exposure overnight | Markets can gap against you |
| Disabling risk settings | This is like removing seatbelts |
