# Dashboard

## Purpose

The Dashboard is your **home screen**. It shows everything important at a single glance — what the AI thinks, what the market is doing, and whether there are any warnings.

**Think of it like the instrument panel in a car.** You do not need to understand every gauge. You just need to know when something is wrong.

---

## Who Should Use It

Everyone. This is the first page you see when you open the app.

---

## What You See

### 1. Main Chart (Center)

A price chart showing how the market is moving.

| Element | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Green candles | Price went UP during that minute |
| Red candles | Price went DOWN during that minute |
| Lines on the chart | AI-drawn support/resistance levels |

**Don't worry if this looks confusing.** The AI reads it for you.

### 2. AI Decision Panel (Right Side)

The most important part of the screen.

| Item | What It Tells You |
|---|---|
| **Decision** | BUY, SELL, or NO TRADE |
| **Confidence** | How sure the AI is (0-100%) |
| **Score** | Quality of the setup (0-100) |
| **Reasoning** | Why the AI made this decision |

**When to pay attention:**

| Color | What It Means |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Green | AI is confident. Good setup. |
| 🟡 Yellow | Moderate confidence. Be careful. |
| 🔴 Red | Low confidence or warning. Do not trade. |

### 3. Risk Status

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 🟢 Low Risk | Normal conditions |
| 🟡 Medium Risk | Be more careful than usual |
| 🟠 High Risk | Consider waiting |
| 🔴 Extreme Risk | Do NOT trade |

### 4. Market Regime

This tells you what KIND of market it is right now:

| Regime | What It Means |
|---|---|
| Strong Bull Trend | Market going up strongly — good for buying |
| Strong Bear Trend | Market going down strongly — good for selling |
| Sideways Range | Market not moving much — wait for breakout |
| High Volatility | Prices moving wildly — be very careful |
| Low Volatility | Very quiet — may not be worth trading |

---

## Cards and Widgets

### Metric Cards

Small boxes showing important numbers.

| Card | When Green | When Red |
|---|---|---|
| Confidence | Above 80% | Below 40% |
| Score | Above 70 | Below 40 |
| Risk | LOW or MEDIUM | HIGH or EXTREME |
| Market Regime | Bullish trending | Illiquid or news-driven |

**These update every 30 seconds automatically.**

---

## Common Beginner Mistakes

| Mistake | Why It's Wrong |
|---|---|
| Focusing only on the chart | The AI panel is more important |
| Ignoring the Risk level | High risk means high chance of loss |
| Trading when AI says NO TRADE | The AI has reasons you cannot see |
| Changing symbols too fast | Let the AI analyze each one |

---

## Pro Tips

- **Start your day** by looking at the Dashboard for 2 minutes
- **Check the Regime** first — it tells you what strategy works today
- **Read the Reasoning** — it teaches you about the market
- **If something is RED**, understand why before doing anything else
