# Welcome to MarketMind AI

## What is This?

MarketMind AI is a **smart trading assistant** that watches the market for you, tells you when it sees a good opportunity, and helps you make better trading decisions.

Think of it like having a team of experts sitting next to you:

- A **Market Analyst** who watches price movements all day
- A **Risk Manager** who makes sure you don't take dangerous trades
- A **Performance Coach** who tracks your results and helps you improve
- A **Quality Inspector** who checks every trade before it happens

The best part? The AI does ALL the hard work. You just need to understand what it's telling you.

## What Makes This Different?

**Most trading platforms** show you charts and expect you to figure everything out.

**MarketMind AI** watches the charts FOR you, makes a DECISION, and EXPLAINS why.

You don't need to know what RSI, MACD, or VWAP mean. The AI knows them. It tells you what matters.

## The Four Safety Layers

Before the AI can place ANY trade, it must pass ALL four checks:

| Layer | Question It Answers |
|---|---|
| **Phase 55 — Safety Check** | "Can we execute this trade safely?" (max 1 share, ₹10,000) |
| **Phase 56 — Quality Check** | "Should we take this trade at all?" (confidence, grade, approval) |
| **Phase 57 — Performance Review** | "How did similar trades perform?" (historical analytics) |
| **Phase 58 — Market Regime** | "What kind of market is this?" (trending, range, volatile) |

All four must say YES before any trade happens.
