# Glossary

Simple explanations of every term used in the application.

---

## A

**AI Confidence (0-100%)** — How sure the AI is about its decision. Higher = more reliable.

**AI Decision** — What the AI thinks you should do: BUY, SELL, or NO TRADE.

**Approval Gates (7 gates)** — Seven conditions the AI checks before allowing a trade. All must pass.

**ATR (Average True Range)** — A number that tells you how much a price typically moves. Higher ATR = more volatile.

**Archived (Model Status)** — A model that has been retired and is no longer in use.

---

## B

**BOS (Break of Structure)** — When price breaks through a previous high or low, suggesting the trend is continuing.

**Breakout** — When price breaks through a support or resistance level. Can signal the start of a new trend.

**BUY Signal** — The AI thinks the price will go up.

---

## C

**Calibration** — How well the AI's confidence matches reality. A well-calibrated AI wins as often as it expects to.

**Calmar Ratio** — A measure of return relative to drawdown. Higher = better risk-adjusted returns.

**Canary** — A small test trade. Named after "canary in a coal mine."

**Candlestick** — A way of showing price movement on a chart. Shows open, high, low, and close prices.

**Champion Model** — The current best AI model, actively making trading decisions.

**CHoCH (Change of Character)** — When the market's behavior changes, suggesting a possible trend reversal.

**Confidence Grade** — Letter grade for AI confidence: VERY_HIGH, HIGH, MODERATE, LOW, VERY_LOW.

**Controlled Live** — Real trading with strict limits: 1 share max, ₹10,000 max per trade.

---

## D

**Daily Loss Limit** — Maximum amount you can lose in a day. Trading stops when hit.

**Dashboard** — Your home screen showing key information at a glance.

**Drawdown** — How far your account has fallen from its highest point. A 20% drawdown means you've lost 20% from the peak.

**Draft (Model Status)** — A new model that hasn't been tested yet.

---

## E

**ECE (Expected Calibration Error)** — A number showing how much the AI's confidence differs from reality. Lower = better.

**EMA (Exponential Moving Average)** — A line on the chart showing average price, giving more weight to recent prices.

**Event Bus** — Internal messaging system that carries data between different parts of the platform.

**Excellent (Trade Score)** — The highest outcome classification (85-100). Perfect trade execution.

**Exposure** — How much of your capital is at risk in open trades.

---

## F

**Failed (Trade Score)** — The lowest outcome classification (0-24). The trade lost money.

**Fake Breakout** — When price briefly breaks a level then quickly reverses. A trap for traders.

**False Signal** — A signal that looks like a trading opportunity but isn't. The AI detects and rejects these.

---

## G

**Good (Trade Score)** — A solid trade (65-84). Could be slightly improved.

**Governance Score** — A number (0-100) showing how well-governed a model is. Higher = better.

---

## H

**High Volatility** — Market condition where prices move a lot, very quickly.

**Higher Timeframe (HTF)** — Longer time periods like 1-hour or 1-day charts. Used to confirm the overall trend.

---

## I-J

**Illiquid Market** — Very few people trading. Hard to enter or exit positions at good prices.

**Incident** — A problem or unusual event that needs attention.

**Institutional Bias** — The overall direction big institutions (banks, funds) seem to be trading.

---

## K

**Kill Switch** — Emergency stop button. When activated, ALL trading stops immediately.

---

## L

**Liquidity** — How easy it is to buy or sell without affecting the price. High liquidity = easy to trade.

**Low Volatility** — Very quiet market with small price movements.

---

## M

**MACD (Moving Average Convergence Divergence)** — An indicator showing trend direction and momentum.

**MAE (Maximum Adverse Excursion)** — The furthest a trade went against you before recovering.

**Market Regime** — The current "personality" of the market (trending up, trending down, ranging, etc.)

**MCE (Maximum Calibration Error)** — The worst-case calibration error across all confidence levels.

**MFE (Maximum Favorable Excursion)** — The furthest a trade went in your favor before closing.

**Model Lineage** — The family tree of AI models — which model came from which.

**Momentum** — How strongly the price is moving in a direction.

---

## N

**NO TRADE** — The AI's decision when conditions aren't favorable for trading.

**Notional** — The total value of a trade (price × quantity). In Controlled Live, capped at ₹10,000.

---

## O

**Opening Auction** — The first 30 minutes of trading. Prices can be erratic.

**Operational State** — Whether the system is healthy, degraded, or halted.

**Outcome Classification** — How a trade performed: Excellent, Good, Average, Poor, or Failed.

**Overconfident (Bias)** — The AI thinks it's more right than it actually is.

---

## P

**P&L (Profit and Loss)** — How much money you've made or lost.

**Paper Trading** — Trading with fake money using real market data.

**Pattern** — A recognizable shape in price movements that might predict future direction.

**Phase 43 Lock** — A master safety lock that prevents unrestricted live trading.

**Poor (Trade Score)** — Below expectations (25-44). Something needs improvement.

**Profit Factor** — Gross profit divided by gross loss. Above 1.0 = profitable. Above 1.5 = good.

**Pullback** — A temporary move against the main trend. Often a good entry point.

---

## Q-R

**R-Multiple** — How many times your risk you made or lost. Making 2R means you made 2× what you risked.

**Recovery Factor** — How quickly the system recovers from problems.

**Regime** — Alternative term for Market Regime (see above).

**REJECT (Trade Grade)** — The AI determined this is NOT a valid trade.

**Replay** — Playing back historical market data to test strategies.

**Resistance** — A price level where selling pressure tends to stop upward movement.

**Risk/Reward Ratio** — Potential loss vs potential gain. 1:2 means risk 1 to make 2.

**Risk Score (0-100)** — Overall risk level. Higher = more dangerous.

**Rollback** — Switching back to a previous version of a model.

**RSI (Relative Strength Index)** — An indicator showing whether a price is overbought (too high) or oversold (too low).

---

## S

**SELL Signal** — The AI thinks the price will go down.

**Sharpe Ratio** — Risk-adjusted return. Higher = better returns for the amount of risk taken.

**Sideways Range** — Price bouncing between two levels without trending.

**Slippage** — The difference between the expected price of a trade and the actual price.

**Sortino Ratio** — Similar to Sharpe, but only considers downside risk.

**Stability (Regime)** — How stable the current market regime is. Higher = more reliable.

**Stop Loss (SL)** — A price level where you exit a trade to prevent further losses.

**Strong Bear Trend** — Market going down strongly and consistently.

**Strong Bull Trend** — Market going up strongly and consistently.

**Support** — A price level where buying pressure tends to stop downward movement.

---

## T

**Target** — The price level where you expect to take profit.

**Trade Grade** — Quality score for a trade setup: A+, A, B, C, D, or REJECT.

**Trade Plan** — The AI's detailed plan for a trade: entry, stop loss, target, risk/reward.

**Trend** — The general direction of the market (up, down, or sideways).

**Trend Following** — A strategy that aims to profit by following the current trend.

---

## U

**Underconfident (Bias)** — The AI is more accurate than it thinks it is.

**Unified Status** — Overall system health: Healthy, Degraded, or Halted.

---

## V

**Validation (Model Status)** — A model currently being tested.

**VIX** — A measure of expected market volatility. High VIX = high uncertainty.

**Volatility** — How much and how quickly prices move.

**VWAP (Volume-Weighted Average Price)** — The average price weighted by trading volume. Used to determine fair value.

---

## W

**WAIT** — The AI needs more information before making a decision.

**Walk-Forward** — A testing method that validates a model across multiple time periods.

**WebSocket** — Technology that pushes live data to your screen without refreshing.

**Win Rate** — Percentage of trades that were profitable. 60% means 6 out of 10 trades won.
