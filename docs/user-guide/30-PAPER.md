# Paper Trading Dashboard

## Purpose

Paper Trading lets you trade with **fake money** using **real market prices**. It's the safest way to learn the system and practice trading.

**Think of it like a flight simulator for pilots.** You practice landing the plane before flying a real one. If you crash in the simulator, nobody gets hurt. If you crash paper trading, you learn a lesson for free.

---

## Who Should Use It

| User | Why |
|---|---|
| **Beginners** | Start here. Do not skip this step. |
| **Intermediate** | Test new strategies before using real money. |
| **Advanced** | Validate AI model changes. |

---

## Features

### Account Overview

| Item | What It Shows |
|---|---|
| **Equity** | Total value of your paper account |
| **Available Cash** | How much you can use for new trades |
| **Total P&L** | How much you've made or lost overall |
| **Return %** | Percentage return on your paper capital |
| **Open Positions** | How many trades are currently open |
| **Closed Trades** | Total completed trades |
| **Win Rate** | % of trades that were profitable |
| **Used Margin** | How much capital is tied up in open trades |

### Controls

| Button | What It Does |
|---|---|
| **Start** | Begins paper trading. The AI starts monitoring. |
| **Pause** | Pauses new trade generation. Existing trades continue. |
| **Resume** | Resumes trade generation after pause. |
| **Stop** | Stops everything. No more paper trades. |
| **Reset** | Resets your paper account to initial state. |

### Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Positions** | All currently open trades |
| **Orders** | Pending and filled orders |
| **Trades** | Complete trade history |
| **Events** | System events and alerts |

---

## How to Use

1. Go to **Paper Trading** in the sidebar
2. Click **Start**
3. Go to **Dashboard** and wait for AI signals
4. Review each signal in **AI Decision Center**
5. Watch trades appear in the **Positions** tab
6. Review completed trades in the bottom panel

---

## Common Mistakes

| Mistake | Why |
|---|---|
| Not treating paper money seriously | Bad habits carry over to real trading |
| Ignoring trades | Learning requires active participation |
| Only looking at winners | Losers teach you more |
| Skipping paper trading | Going straight to live is reckless |
